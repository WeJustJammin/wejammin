## Formula

Existing AC001-AC048 are unchanged. AC005-AC034 already own the six generic obligations of each of CMS-03B-05 through CMS-03B-09 (contract and happy path, validation, authority, idempotency and CAS, failure mapping, audit and outbox), AC001-AC004 and AC035-AC043 own the locked IA behavior, and AC046-AC048 own the DEC-122 step-up receiving criteria. DEC-148 appended operation ownership to AC035 (CMS-03B-15, 16, 17, 18), AC038 (CMS-03B-20) and AC041 (CMS-03B-19) without adding criteria and deferred the full cascade to Slice 11 setup.

1. DEC-149 resolves the cascade as additive, following the Slice 10 DEC-133 precedent (5 operations x 6 classes = 30): each of the six operations the Slice 11 specification cascade added contributes six non-overlapping obligations (contract/shape, validation, authority/privacy, runtime bounds, safe failure, consumer/verification). 6 x 6 = 36.
2. DEC-154 applies the same additive rule to every other obligation of the Slice 11 specification cascade (2026-10-07) that no criterion named, one obligation per criterion with an exact section citation: derived revision state (2), frozen manifest (4), settings snapshot (2), preflight registry (5), accessibility checker (5), time authority (4), publisher authority and separation of duties (1), reviewer decision (3), review invalidation (3), publication lineage (3), preview token (2) and persistence (4) = 38. These rows replace no existing row: the generic per-operation rows cite sections at heading level and name none of these obligations.

## Omitted classes

None. Every one of the 36 (operation, class) cells has specification text and is cited in the Lines column. Two cells are narrower than their name suggests and are kept because the specification still fixes a testable obligation there:

- CMS-03B-19 and CMS-03B-20 have no browser surface (FE03:560 and BE03b:174 forbid one), so their consumer/verification cell is the internal consumer (the Shard 04 preview route for CMS-03B-19; the Worker scheduled handler and the CMS-03B-15 schedule summary for CMS-03B-20) plus absence from the browser route inventory, not a UI.
- CMS-03B-19 runtime bounds is the read-safe, zero-write contract plus the declared seam timings, because the 500 ms timeout, retries at 75 ms and 150 ms and 30-second circuit are applied by the BE04c caller (BE03b:178, BE04c:88).

Not added as separate rows because the specification gives them no obligation beyond the generic per-operation rows: the E4/E9 audience grammar and the E5/E6 operands (generic validation and idempotency/CAS rows AC005-AC034, step-up rows AC046-AC048 and AC071), the write-path lock order (owned by the Slice 09 and Slice 10 writers and the 03a activation), and the CMS-03B-08 version-set recompute (AC023-AC028).
