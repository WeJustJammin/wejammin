## Counting conventions

- Each BE operation contributes one happy-path row (two for CMS-03A-14 create and revoke, CFG-05B-06 completed and reconciling), one row per request field x constraint including the mutation or read headers, one row per distinct validation message, one row per HTTP status in its error matrix (401 UNAUTHENTICATED and 401 STEP_UP_REQUIRED are separate; 502, 503 and 504 are separate), one row per allowed and denied principal, one row per ownership, idempotency, concurrency and rollback rule and one row for its rate rule.
- A rule stated in both an error cell and a field or scoping constraint is counted once, at the field or scoping row; the matching status is counted once at the error row.
- BE03a Route Registry states the per-user and per-party rate numbers for every operation (30 and 60 per minute on CMS-03A-09 to 12, 10 and 20 on CMS-03A-04 and CMS-03A-14 to 17, 120 and 240 on CMS-03A-06, 07, 13 and 18); each rate row therefore states both numbers.
- Dependency failures use the single BE00 code DEPENDENCY_UNAVAILABLE for 502 (invalid response), 503 (unavailable or circuit open) and 504 (registered deadline exceeded); the BE03b, BE03c and FE03 spellings DEPENDENCY_INVALID_RESPONSE and DEPENDENCY_DEADLINE_EXCEEDED are a recorded naming conflict, not codes counted here.
- A role-by-feature matrix row is counted once per hidden, locked or read-only cell as well as the full cell (formula line 34); the CMS capability grant console row therefore counts seven not-rendered persona cells and one full Admin cell.
- OD-4 rows count only the BE03a, FE03 and IA03 Slice 09 content (CMS-03A-01 and CMS-03A-09 locale configuration, CMS-03A-04 hash evidence, compatibility, protected detail, persistence, the authoring controls and the Slice 09 IA edge case); the CMS-03C-04 fallback-chain equality expectation, the 409 FALLBACK_CHAIN_MISMATCH handling, the locale variant form and the CMS-03B-10 locale-set reading stay in Slices 10 and 12.
- A removed row is never tombstoned: the amendment IDs are renumbered contiguously from AC284 after each correction pass, and every guard and cross-reference is regenerated from the ledger.
- Rows for CMS-03A-04 count only the amendment delta (STEP_UP_REQUIRED, activator-only capability, approvalIds meaning, activator-only MFA recheck, authority recheck, specialist drift, advance-only plan, CMS-owned approval evidence); AC085-AC102 keep covering the unchanged activation contract.
- BE03b and BE03c consumed boundaries count only the integration rows and the service-only template resolver; the CMS-03B-10, CMS-03B-11, CMS-03B-01 and CMS-03C-01 items stay in Slices 10 and 12.
- DEC-120 rows state the 90 UTC day standing-grant ceiling (validThrough at most today plus 89); DEC-108 schema-review assignments keep the seven day bound.
